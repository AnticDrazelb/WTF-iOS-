# WTF Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

WTF is an app that listens while you've turned it on, so it can tell you what you were just talking about.

## What WTF records
While listening is on, WTF records speech it hears through your iPhone's microphone (or your AirPods, if you choose), including other people's voices. Silence and music aren't kept.

## Where it goes
Nowhere. Speech is transcribed, summarised and searched on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device speech recognition and Apple Intelligence. WTF has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it collects no data. WTF's data is excluded from iCloud and computer backups.

WTF downloads models it needs (speech recognition, a speech detector and voice models) once. These downloads don't include anything about you.

## What's kept
- Recordings are deleted once their conversation is over.
- Transcripts, summaries and suggestions are kept for the time you choose in Settings › Keep Transcripts (a week to start with).
- Voiceprints of people you name, the lists you keep, the facts WTF has learnt and the words you've corrected stay until you delete them.

## Contacts and Calendar
Only if you turn them on: names from your contacts and calendar help WTF hear them correctly, and appointments it hears are checked against your calendar. Nothing is copied off your iPhone.

## Deleting
Delete any conversation, list item, person or fact in the app, or everything in Settings › Delete All Data. Deleting WTF deletes all of it.

## Recording other people
Let people know when WTF is listening, and follow the law where you are on recording conversations.
